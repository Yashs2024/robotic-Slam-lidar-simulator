PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. DATA COLLECTION PROTOCOLS

At VectraSim, we prioritize your privacy. The RoboAI Suite operates largely client-side. However, to enhance system capabilities, we may collect the following telemetry:

  • Session Data: Local preferences and simulation states (stored locally via localStorage or sessionStorage).
  • Authentication Data: Email addresses and hashed credentials if you create an operator account on our Hub.
  • Usage Analytics: Anonymized interaction logs to optimize WebGL performance and algorithm efficiency.

2. TELEMETRY & COOKIE USAGE

Cookies are utilized strictly to store local simulator preferences and session telemetry. By interacting with our platform, you consent to this localized data processing in compliance with standard privacy protocols (GDPR/CCPA compliant). You can configure your consent preferences via the persistent Cookie Banner on our landing pages.

3. DATA ENCRYPTION

All authentication communications (if utilizing the cloud Hub) are encrypted using industry-standard TLS 1.3. Your simulation data (SLAM maps, Kinematic trajectories, and Swarm logs) remains on your local machine unless explicitly synchronized to your cloud workspace.

4. THIRD-PARTY INTEGRATIONS

We do not sell or rent your data to third-party data brokers. Analytics or external API services (such as Supabase for authentication) strictly adhere to their respective data processing agreements.

5. OPERATOR RIGHTS

You reserve the right to request access, rectification, or erasure of your cloud-stored operator data. You can clear your local cache at any time to erase local simulation telemetry.

If you have any questions, please reach out to us at yashusurve2005@gmail.com.
