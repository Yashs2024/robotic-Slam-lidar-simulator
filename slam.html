🚀 What to Try First
  1. ▶ Launch Sim — Hit the button in the top bar to start the robot & LiDAR
  2. 🎯 Click scene — Click anywhere on the 3D view to set an autonomous waypoint
  3. 🔊 Raise Noise % — Drag the Noise slider to ~15 and watch SLAM accuracy degrade
  4. 📷 Switch Camera — Press C to cycle Top-Down → Follow → Free
  5. 🧮 A* vs Dijkstra — Switch algorithms in the Pathfinding panel, then click a new waypoint to compare routes
  6. 🌀 Try Maze map — Load the Maze preset and enable Frontier Exploration (auto-mapping)
💡 Tip: Enable EKF + MCL together to see sensor fusion battle odometry drift
